GlucoTwin — system architecture

A patient-specific type 2 diabetes digital twin. Four input streams are harmonised onto one causal timeline, fused by the model layer, calibrated into prediction intervals and adverse-event probabilities, and served to two clinician interfaces. Raw patient records never leave the clinic.

Health record

Age, sex, BMI, HbA1c, fasting glucose, eGFR, diagnoses, medications, genetic markers. Synthea-ready.

Real · open

CGM · 145 patients

CGMacros (45 adults, PhysioNet) and Shanghai T2DM (100 patients, figshare). Removing this stream hurts the forecast most of all.

Real + simulated

Wearable

Fitbit heart rate and activity; sleep, HRV and steps simulated. At +60 min: +0.04 mg/dL, CI includes zero — reported, not hidden.

Real + presets

Meals

Logged macronutrients, plus 16 Indian presets from IFCT-2017. Meals are the largest single gain.

One causal timeline

Common grid. Every cohort resampled to a single 5-minute schema with a shared column set; loader checksums verified.

Causal features only. No feature may use information from after the prediction instant — enforced in code and covered by tests.

Patient-grouped splits. Training, inner-validation, calibration and holdout patients never overlap, so no patient appears on both sides of an evaluation.

Gap and quality handling. Sensor gaps flagged rather than silently interpolated; a per-window quality score travels with the forecast.

Fused feature set. Recent CGM history and its slope · time of day · meal macronutrients · heart rate and activity · the static profile.

src/data · src/features

Fusion forecaster

Tuned LightGBM on the change from the current value at +30, +60 and +120 min. The served model.

Mechanistic twin

A per-patient glucose–insulin ODE. RK4 integration, extended Kalman filter state. Also supplies the features used by the hybrids.

Hybrid physics + ML

Twin-derived features fed to the forecaster, and a GRU trained with an ODE-consistency loss.

Personalisation

Fine-tuning or recursive least squares on the patient's own days: about 1 mg/dL better after 3 to 5 days.

Federated training

FedAvg and FedProx across simulated clinics. Five-clinic and two-site layouts, plus a privacy noise sweep.

80% intervals

Split-conformal and CV+. Coverage 80.8% (CGMacros) and 79.5% (Shanghai) at +60 min, per subgroup as well as pooled.

Event probabilities

Platt and isotonic calibration for crossing 180, 140 and 70 mg/dL. Calibrated means 40% happens about 40% of the time.

Robustness & quality

Faults injected, causal features recomputed, scored on the same anchors. Quality score: completeness, recency, plausibility, rate of change.

Alerts & explainability

Alert-burden curves let a clinic trade sensitivity against false alarms explicitly. SHAP attribution over the fused feature set explains each forecast.

Forecast + band

Trajectory to +120 min with its 80% interval and one plain sentence. The band widens with the horizon — that widening is the content.

Adverse-event risk

Calibrated chance of hyperglycaemia or hypoglycaemia inside the window, at the threshold the clinic chose. Thresholds follow the 2019 consensus.

What-if answers

Counterfactual meal size, carbohydrate, timing, an Indian preset swap, and a post-meal walk — through the monotone model and the twin.

Trust surface

Sensor data quality, personalisation depth, input tier available, feature attribution, and the limits printed beside each answer.

Web console  web/

A single-page clinician console. Patient list with triage ordering, then per patient: forecast, what-if lab, glucose report (AGP, time in range, GMI per the 2019 consensus), health record, sleep and activity context, visit summary, and an evidence section carrying every cohort-level result.

Role-gated access — clinician and care coordinator see different fields — with a session access log. Dark and light themes, mg/dL ⇄ mmol/L, readable at tablet width. The demo runs on 120 simulated patients, models applied zero-shot.

Streamlit application  make app

Ten pages: Overview, Live twin, Glucose report, What-if lab, Twin state, Personalization, Input tiers, Data quality, Evidence, and a PDF export for the patient file.

Runs locally; no data leaves the machine. A units switch shows mg/dL or mmol/L, and the Evidence page reproduces every cohort-level table and figure.

Both interfaces read the same bundle, so they never disagree on a number. Every page carries the decision-support disclaimer: the system never recommends a dose or a drug.

Method & evaluation

  • Pre-registered. Protocol, candidates and decision rule written to docs/DECISIONS.md before the run. 70+ entries.
  • Patient-grouped CV with repeats, an inner validation split for every choice, and a reserved holdout the runner refuses to reuse.
  • Baselines it must beat: persistence, per-patient autoregression, ridge, a GRU.
  • Paired patient bootstrap intervals — over patients, never over time windows.
  • Also scored: coverage, event AUROC, alert burden, plausibility, 87 matched breakfasts, input-tier ablations; stressed by transfer, faults and a synthetic Indian cohort. Nulls published at full size.

AI / ML models and training

  • Inputs → target. Causal features per anchor: CGM lags, rolling statistics, least-squares slope and acceleration, time-of-day sin/cos, meal macros, heart rate and activity, static profile. Target is the change from the current value.
  • Served forecaster. LightGBM, one head per horizon, trained with an absolute-error loss to match the metric it is scored on. 16 candidates, best three averaged, on inner-validation patients only.
  • Sequence model. PyTorch GRU over the causal window with FiLM static conditioning; also the backbone of the physics-informed hybrid.
  • Constrained what-if. A second LightGBM with monotonicity constraints: less carbohydrate can never predict a higher peak, by construction.
  • Uncertainty. Split-conformal and CV+ intervals; Platt and isotonic probability calibration.

Privacy by design

  • Anonymised open or synthetic data only; no real patient record is in the repository.
  • Federated: FedAvg matches pooled training (difference 0.09–0.12 mg/dL, intervals include zero) and beats a lone small clinic by ≈2 mg/dL.
  • Data minimisation by design: only model updates are exchanged. No formal differential-privacy guarantee is claimed at this clinic count.
  • Role-gated access with a session log; mapped to India's DPDP Act in docs/limitations.md.

Engineering & scope

  • Python 3.11 · LightGBM · PyTorch · SHAP · Streamlit. Runs on a laptop CPU; no GPU required.
  • One make target per stage; everything regenerates from source. CI runs ruff, black, pytest and a smoke run. Docker image, datasheet and model card included.
  • One condition: type 2 diabetes. Decision support only — not a medical device.
  • India, sleep and federated results are simulations: they test design, not clinical performance (sleep: ≈0.06 mg/dL, ≈9,000 patient-days to detect). Not prospectively validated.
