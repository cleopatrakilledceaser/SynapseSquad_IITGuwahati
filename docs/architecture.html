GlucoTwin — system architecture

A patient-specific type 2 diabetes digital twin. Four input streams are harmonised onto one causal timeline, fused by the model layer, calibrated into intervals and event probabilities, and served to two clinician interfaces. Raw records never leave the clinic.

Fusion forecaster

Tuned LightGBM predicting the change from the current value at +30, +60 and +120 min. The served model.

Mechanistic twin

A per-patient glucose–insulin ODE. RK4 integration, extended Kalman filter for the state.

Personalisation

The population model plus fine-tuning, or recursive least squares on the patient's own days.

Federated training

FedAvg and FedProx across clinics. Only model updates cross the boundary.

80% intervals

Split-conformal and CV+. Measured coverage 80.8% on CGMacros and 79.5% on Shanghai at +60 min.

Event probabilities

Platt and isotonic calibration for crossing 180, 140 and 70 mg/dL. Probabilities, never bare labels.

Alert policy

Burden curves: episodes caught per false alarm per day, so a clinic chooses its own threshold.

Forecast + band

Trajectory to +120 min with its 80% interval and one plain sentence.

Adverse-event risk

Calibrated chance of hyper- or hypoglycaemia inside the window.

What-if answers

Counterfactual meal size, carbohydrate, timing and a post-meal walk.

Trust surface

Data quality, personalisation depth, SHAP attribution, and the limits beside each answer.

Web console  web/

A single-page clinician console: patient list with triage, forecast, what-if lab, glucose report (AGP, time in range, GMI), health record, sleep and activity context, visit summary, and a role-gated access log with a session audit trail.

Dark and light themes · mg/dL ⇄ mmol/L · readable at tablet width.

Streamlit application  make app

Ten pages: Overview, Live twin, Glucose report, What-if lab, Twin state, Personalization, Input tiers, Data quality, Evidence, and a PDF export for the patient file.

Every page carries the decision-support disclaimer. The clinician remains the decision maker: the system never recommends a dose or a drug.

Method — how every number was obtained

  • Pre-registered. Each protocol and decision rule written to docs/DECISIONS.md before the run that tested it. Over 70 entries.
  • Paired patient bootstrap. Confidence intervals computed over patients, never over time windows — readings from one patient are not independent.
  • Untouched holdout. Final claims confirmed on patients never used for any choice anywhere in the project.
  • Null results published. Where a signal does not help, the number is reported at full size rather than dropped.

Privacy by design

  • Anonymised open or synthetic data only. No real patient record enters the repository.
  • Federated: FedAvg matches pooled central training (difference 0.09 to 0.12 mg/dL, intervals include zero) and beats a single small clinic alone by about 2 mg/dL.
  • Role-gated clinician access with a session access log.
  • Data handling mapped to India's DPDP Act in docs/limitations.md.

Scope and limits

  • One condition: type 2 diabetes.
  • Decision support only — never a dose or a drug. Not a medical device.
  • The India, sleep and federated results are simulations: they test design, not clinical performance.
  • Not prospectively validated.
